# AI research and reports

Research requires an explicit retrieval source. Preserve title, publisher/domain, URL, publication/update date and retrieval time. Validate citations and map material claims to source references. Separate sourced facts, synthesis, assumptions and unknowns.

If retrieval is unavailable, say so. Model-only completion is not live research and must not receive fabricated citations.

Reports include question/scope, date, data snapshot, method, sources, findings, limitations, uncertainty, provider/model metadata and review status. Private roster data never enters a shared knowledge base.
